The challenge

What was holding bookings back

Coastal Luxe Aesthetics wanted to elevate its online presence and attract new clients for its premium aesthetic treatments. The clinic needed a website that reflected its luxurious brand, made it easy for clients to book appointments, and gave detailed information about every service.

Our approach

What we did

Website

Designed and built the clinic's website.

SEO & local search

Ongoing SEO with regular reporting, tracking the clinic's Google rankings across two locations.

Social media

Managing the clinic's social media pages and content.

The outcome

What changed for the clinic

The new site brought higher client engagement, with more inquiries about advanced aesthetic treatments, and stronger online visibility that led to a rise in new clients. In Aubry Ann's words, she has gained clients from the work, and the clinic's social media pages have been transformed.
