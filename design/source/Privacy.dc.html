Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Digital Services Master collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Digital Services Master (“we”, “us”, “our”) is a web development and digital marketing agency serving clinics and businesses in the Philippines and Australia.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you choose to give us, for example when you book a Clinic Growth Audit or contact us:

  • Your name, clinic or business name, email address and phone number
  • Your website address and the services you're interested in
  • Anything you tell us in a message

We also collect some information automatically when you visit, such as your browser type, device, pages viewed and approximate location, through cookies and analytics tools.

3. How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare your audit
  • To provide and manage the services you engage us for
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it
  • To send you updates or marketing, only where you've agreed to receive them

4. Cookies & analytics

We may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how our site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, although some parts of the site may not work as intended.

5. Sharing your information

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with trusted providers who help us run our website and business (such as hosting, email and analytics services), or where we're required to by law.

6. Storage & security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorised access, and we keep it only for as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required by law.

7. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to marketing at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Philippines) or the Privacy Act 1988 (Australia).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

9. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email us at digitalservicesmaster@gmail.com.
